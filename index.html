Yardwise — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 November 2026

Yardwise is a shunting puzzle game for iPhone, made by Maxim Cucerenco. This policy explains what the game does, and does not do, with your data.

Your progress: kept on your device

Everything Yardwise keeps about you (the levels you have cleared, your stars, your hints and your settings) is stored on your iPhone. If iCloud is on, a copy is also kept in your own iCloud account, so your progress follows you to another iPhone or a reinstall; Apple stores that copy for you. So that hints used on two iPhones are counted correctly, the copy includes Apple’s identifier for this app on each of your devices; it stays in your save. Yardwise has no server of its own, so the developer cannot see your save. Reset progress in the game’s Settings erases your stars from the device and, when you are signed in to iCloud, from iCloud; your purchases and hints are kept.

The game does not ask for access to your camera, photos, contacts, location or microphone.

What Yardwise itself collects: game analytics

To learn which levels are too hard and where players give up, Yardwise uses Google Analytics for Firebase. It records how the game is played: levels started, solved or left, the moves made against the level’s par, and the help used, as well as purchases made in the game. Google also records basic app events (such as the app opening and how long it is used), an approximate location derived from your IP address, and a random identifier for this installation of the app. The game sets no name, account or user identifier, does not collect your device’s advertising identifier, and sends Google no signals for ad personalisation. The developer sees these figures only in totals and reports. Google processes this data under Firebase’s privacy and security terms.

Where the law asks for your consent first (in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), analytics start only if you agree, and you can change your choice at any time in the game’s Settings.

Yardwise is built with the Unity engine. Unity’s own analytics, crash reporting and performance reporting are switched off in the game. The Unity runtime may still send basic anonymous technical information about the device, such as its model, operating system version and hardware capabilities, as described in Unity’s privacy policy.

Game Center

Yardwise signs you in to Apple’s Game Center if you use it. While you are signed in, the game sends Game Center your scores on the Daily Jam and the Weekly puzzle for their leaderboards, and the achievements you earn. Other Game Center players may see your Game Center nickname and scores on those leaderboards, depending on your Game Center settings. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy; the developer does not receive your Game Center details.

Advertising

This version of Yardwise shows no ads and contains no advertising SDK. If a later version adds ads, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Purchases

Yardwise offers optional purchases: hint packs and a one-time Pro purchase. They are sold through Apple’s In-App Purchase system; Apple handles the payment, and the developer never sees or stores your payment details. The game keeps each purchase’s transaction number in your save on the device (and in your iCloud copy of it), only so that a purchase is never delivered twice or lost.

Children

Yardwise has no chat, no accounts of its own and no links to social networks. The developer does not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects this policy, such as ads, a new online feature, or analytics collecting something new, this page is updated and the effective date above changes with it.
