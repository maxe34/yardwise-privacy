Yardwise — Support

Help with the Yardwise shunting puzzle, on iPhone

Contact

Found a bug, a level that looks wrong, or have an idea? Write to cucerenco.m23@gmail.com. Please mention your iPhone model, the yard and the level number.

Common questions

How do I play? Build the departing train on the main line, the track on the left, in the order its job card shows, front car at the top. Tap a car to fetch it with the shunter, then tap a track to park it there. From Tyne Marshalling on, the shunter can fetch up to three cars at once, and tapping the shunter turns the cars it holds round. Fewer moves win more stars, and Undo is always free.

I’m stuck. Hint rings the next best move. It uses one hint: you start with three, more come in hint packs, and Pro makes them unlimited. Show run plays the best run from the start. Help never lowers your stars.

I’m out of new levels for today. Ten new levels open each day, and replaying levels you have cleared is always free. Pro removes the daily limit.

Is my progress saved? Yes, on your iPhone. With iCloud on it is also kept in your iCloud, so it comes back on another iPhone or after a reinstall.

I bought Pro on another iPhone. Open Settings and tap Restore purchases. Hints are part of your progress, which follows you through iCloud.
